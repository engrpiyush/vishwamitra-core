Personal advocates — an AI that speaks for one person, grounded in real evidence they chose to share and reviewed themselves. Not a profile, not a chatbot persona: an advocate you can question.

Invited to talk to one?

Every advocate lives at its own address, shared by the person it represents. You'll need an access code from them — it arrives by email and works on their advocate's own page.

You'll be chatting with an AI advocate, not with the person directly; conversations are recorded and visible to them.

Building your own?

Members sign in on their advocate's own address — the one from their invitation. There they share evidence, review what their advocate may say, and manage who gets to talk to it.

Access is invitation-only while we build.

Built from real material

Recordings, documents and endorsements the person contributes — not scraped, not imagined.

Reviewed by the person

They see what was understood and decide what stands before their advocate ever says it.

Honest by construction

Answers stay grounded in the reviewed evidence — an advocate would rather say less than make things up.
