// 01 · the problem

Off-the-shelf AI doesn't know your world.

Rented models are trained on everyone and no one. They don't know your work, your company, or your customers — the knowledge that actually makes you distinct is exactly what they miss, and you never own what you build on top of them.

Generic by default

Foundation models know the average of the internet — not your projects, your people, or your product. The specifics that matter are exactly the ones they blur over.

Your knowledge is trapped

The things that make you or your business distinct live in documents, recordings and people's heads — scattered, unverified, and impossible to hand to a model wholesale.

You rent, you don't own

Wrap someone's API and you're renting intelligence on their terms — repriced, deprecated, or cut off at will. Nothing you can keep, run yourself, or truly call your own.

// 02 · the solution

Not a wrapper. Not a prompt. A model that's yours.

Vishwakarma.ai builds a dedicated model from your own verified knowledge — for a person or a company — and hands you the weights. It's grounded in what's real, candid about what it doesn't know, and yours to keep and run.

// 03 · the method

Four steps from what you know to a model you own.

From your raw knowledge to a live, conversational model you own — verified and auditable at every step.

01_GATHER

Gather

A structured conversation plus your real material — documents, history, recordings, and the endorsements or knowledge only you or your company holds.

02_VERIFY

Verify

Every claim is checked, corroborated, and reviewed before it can be trained in. Nothing is imagined — you decide what stands.

03_TRAIN

Train

Everything is curated into reviewed training data and tuned into a dedicated model on managed, enterprise-grade infrastructure.

04_OWN

Own

The model goes live at a shareable link — and the weights are yours to keep and run, on our infrastructure or your own hardware.

// 06 · faq

The questions we hear first.

A dedicated model, built from your verified material and delivered as something you own: the weights themselves, a live conversational link, and a report of what it was trained on and how it behaves. Run it on our infrastructure or your own hardware — either way, it's yours.

No. A wrapper rents a generic model and puts instructions in front of it — the knowledge stays generic, and the provider owns everything. The factory works differently: your material is verified fact by fact, a dedicated model is tuned on it, its behavior is tested before it ships, and the weights end up in your hands. There's no third party who can reprice or switch your model off.

No — and this is the heart of the design. The model never impersonates anyone. Whether it's a career advocate speaking about a person or a sales rep speaking for a company, it represents — candidly — rather than pretends to be. It's honest about limits, because credibility depends on honesty, not flattery.

The verified-knowledge layer. The interface and the base models can be copied; a corpus of real, consent-verified knowledge — a person's endorsers, or a company's proprietary material — reviewed and turned into training data cannot be acquired quickly. It requires genuine access and a trusted process, which compounds over time into a structural moat.

You pay to build and own your model. Individuals pay to build, host, and update their advocate across their career; businesses pay to build a rep and own its weights, hosted by us or run on their own hardware. Access and serving — per conversation, by subscription, or via enterprise API — layer on top.

Consent is designed in from the first step. Every contributor participates knowingly, every data source is authorised by its owner, and every conversation is logged and auditable. The person or business owns and controls what their model is built from — and, in the end, the weights themselves.
