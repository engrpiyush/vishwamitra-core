Version 1

What vishwakarma.ai is

vishwakarma.ai builds and hosts personal advocates — AI assistants that speak for one person, grounded in evidence that person chose to share and reviewed. Each advocate lives on its own web address and answers questions about the person it represents.

Who these terms cover

These terms apply to members (people who sign in to build and manage their own advocate) and to guests (people invited to talk to an advocate with an access code). By signing in, or by using an access code, you agree to them.

Your evidence is a permanent contribution

When you upload recordings, documents or other material, you confirm at the moment of upload that you have the right to share it and that anyone recorded in it agreed to be recorded for this purpose. Everything you upload permanently becomes part of your advocate's evidence — it is woven into how your advocate understands and represents you, and the service is built on that permanence. Upload only what you are comfortable contributing on those terms.

Talking to an advocate

An advocate is an AI. It is not the person, and it can be imperfect, incomplete or wrong — it speaks for someone, grounded in their evidence, but its answers are generated, not written by them. Don't treat an advocate's answers as the person's own words or as professional advice.

Conversations are recorded

Every conversation with an advocate — including guest conversations — is recorded and is visible to the person the advocate represents and to the people who operate the service. Don't share anything in a conversation you wouldn't want them to see.

Access codes are personal

A guest access code is issued to you, for you. Don't pass it on — the person who invited you chose who they wanted to talk to their advocate.

Fair use

Don't attempt to break, probe or overload the service, to access someone else's advocate or data, to misrepresent an advocate's answers as the person's own statements, or to use the service for anything unlawful. We may suspend access that abuses the service or the people it represents.

Changes to these terms

When these terms change materially, members are asked to accept the new version at their next sign-in before continuing.

Questions

vishwakarma.ai is run by a small team. For anything about these terms, reply to any email you've received from us, or ask the person who invited you.

Privacy policy

Version 1

What we hold

  • Sign-in details — members sign in with Google; we keep the email address and name Google shares with us. We never see your password.
  • Your evidence — the recordings, documents and other material a member uploads to build their advocate, and what the service derives from it.
  • Conversations — every chat with an advocate, kept as part of the advocate's record. Guest conversations are included.
  • Guest emails — a guest's email address is used to send their access code, and (only if the guest asks us to) to tell the member someone wanted to talk while their advocate was offline.

What it's used for

One thing: building, serving and improving the advocate the material belongs to. We don't sell your information, we don't share it for advertising, and there is no tracking or profiling of visitors.

You're talking to an AI

Conversations on this service are with an AI advocate, not with the person it represents. Chats are recorded and visible to that person and to the people who operate the service — this is disclosed on every chat surface, and it is how members hear the questions people bring to their advocate.

Evidence is permanent

Uploaded evidence is a permanent contribution to the advocate it belongs to (see the terms). Chat records are kept for as long as the advocate exists so the member can review them.

Where it lives

The service runs on Google Cloud. Your material is stored there, protected by the same access controls that keep each advocate's world separate from every other.

Questions

For anything about your information, reply to any email you've received from us, or ask the person who invited you.

Cookie policy

Version 1

Two cookies, both functional

  • Session cookie — keeps your visit working: it holds a member's sign-in as they move around their advocate's site, and it protects the forms anyone submits (like entering an access code).
  • Guest session (adv_session) — set when a guest enters an access code; it keeps the conversation going for the length of the visit.

That's the whole list. There are no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies and no third-party cookies of any kind.

On-device preferences

Your browser's local storage remembers small on-device preferences — your light/dark theme choice, and that you've dismissed the cookie notice. These never leave your device.

The notice

Because both cookies are strictly functional, the banner you may see is a notice, not a consent form — the service works exactly the same either way.
